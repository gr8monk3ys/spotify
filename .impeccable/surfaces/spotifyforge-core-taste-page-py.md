# Taste page (`spotifyforge export page`)

Scope: one generated, self-contained HTML file. Visitor mode: Read.

Audience and job: someone arriving from Lorenzo's GitHub or CV with a minute or two, deciding whether the project is real and its author careful. They read; they are asked for nothing.

Content: summary only. Genre-by-decade grid, most-liked artists, most-liked albums, and notes on how the counting works. No track list, no play history. The file is local; publishing is undecided.

Memorable moment: seeing, in one grid, that psychedelic rock lives in the 1960s for this listener and hip hop in the 2010s.

History: a roll first assigned a railway-timetable world (yellow sheet, black ink), which the user chose and then replaced mid-build with "make the design more like lscaturchio.xyz". The user's pin beats the roll. The grid survived the change; the timetable's look did not.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A page from the author's own notebook. The library is a ruled table of genres against decades, set in the site's voice. It refuses the stat-card dashboard: no cards, no hero number, no bars.

OWN-WORLD: lscaturchio.xyz's "Field Notebook", inherited whole: warm paper (#f9f8f5) or night (#111317), ink, 1px sand hairlines, one forest-green pen (#184e35 / #42a979) for emphasis only. Fraunces speaks, Instrument Sans reads, IBM Plex Mono files every count and date as an uppercase wall label.

STORY: The visitor recognises the site they came from, understands what this person listens to and when it was made, believes the numbers because the method section says how they were counted, and may follow the one link to the repository.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A mono wall label (source and compile date). A Fraunces title at display size, the owner in ink and "by genre and decade" in the pen. A muted one-sentence thesis. At the right edge, a 300px column of hairline rows pairing a label with a mono figure. A hairline, then section 01 with its label and a Fraunces heading stating the library's busiest decade.

FORM: The site's own masthead and stacked hairline rows, applied to a data table. Signature interaction: a reading guide, where pointing at a cell tints its row and column. No entrance motion, by the site's rule. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
